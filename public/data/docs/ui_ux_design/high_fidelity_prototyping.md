## Best Practices

- **Start simple, iterate**: Begin with the basics and add complexity gradually.
- **Follow industry standards**: Adhere to established conventions.
- **Document as you learn**: Keep notes organized for future reference.
- **Practice regularly**: Consistent hands-on practice is more effective than passive reading.
- **Stay updated**: Follow official documentation and community resources.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Skipping fundamentals**: Rushing to advanced topics leads to gaps.
- **Copy-pasting without understanding**: Always understand *why* something works.
- **Ignoring documentation**: Official docs are the most accurate resource.
- **Not testing**: Always validate your work through testing.
