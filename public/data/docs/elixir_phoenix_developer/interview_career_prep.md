# Interview & Career Preparation for Elixir/Phoenix Developers

This guide equips you with the knowledge and strategies to excel in technical interviews, effectively communicate your project experiences, articulate your problem-solving approach, and strategically position yourself for Elixir/Phoenix development roles.

## 1. Technical Deep Dive for Elixir/Phoenix Interviews

Technical interviews for Elixir/Phoenix roles often test your understanding of functional programming principles, concurrency models, and the Phoenix framework's conventions and strengths. Be prepared to discuss more than just syntax; focus on *why* Elixir and Phoenix are chosen for specific problems.

### 1.1 Elixir Fundamentals: Processes, OTP, Immutability, Pattern Matching

Understand the core tenets of Elixir:
*   **Immutability**: How it simplifies concurrency and reduces bugs.
*   **Pattern Matching**: Its pervasive use in function heads, `case` statements, and argument destructuring for elegant and robust code.
*   **Processes**: Elixir's lightweight concurrency primitive. Differentiate between OS threads and Elixir processes.
