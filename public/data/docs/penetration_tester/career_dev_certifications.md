## Best Practices

- **Start simple, iterate**: Begin with the basics and add complexity gradually.
- **Follow industry standards**: Adhere to established conventions used by experienced professionals.
- **Document as you learn**: Keep notes and code snippets organized for future reference.
- **Practice regularly**: Consistent hands-on practice is more effective than passive reading.
- **Stay updated**: This field evolves rapidly — follow official documentation and community resources.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Skipping fundamentals**: Rushing to advanced topics without solid basics leads to gaps in understanding.
- **Copy-pasting without understanding**: Always ensure you understand *why* something works, not just *that* it works.
- **Ignoring documentation**: Official docs are often the most accurate and up-to-date resource available.
- **Not testing**: Always validate your work through testing and peer review.
