# Cloud Robotics & Edge AI

> Part of the **Robotics Engineer** roadmap on SkillBun | Difficulty: **Advanced**

Explore the integration of cloud computing and edge AI with robotic systems for offloading heavy computation, fleet management, data storage, and leveraging distributed intelligence. Understand cloud platforms like AWS RoboMaker or Google Cloud Robotics.

## Why This Matters

Understanding cloud robotics & edge ai is a critical step in your journey as a robotics engineer. This topic builds the foundation for practical, real-world skills that employers actively look for.
