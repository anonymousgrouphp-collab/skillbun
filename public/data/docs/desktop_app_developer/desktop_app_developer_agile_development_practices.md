# Agile Development and Team Practices: Study Guide

### Concept 1: Sprints and Releases
Aligning sprint targets with compiler build cycles, testing phases, and store publication timelines.

### Concept 2: Daily Standups & Board Management
Using boards (Jira, Trello) to track development issues, design changes, and QA validation flows.

### Concept 3: Cross-Functional Syncs
Conducting regular syncs between design teams (variables) and development teams (tokens) to prevent design drift.
