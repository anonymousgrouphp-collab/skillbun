# Ranking Systems and Real-time Feature Stores

> Part of the **Recommendation Systems Engineer** roadmap on SkillBun | Difficulty: **Essential**

Design and implement advanced ranking models that take candidates from the retrieval stage and re-rank them for optimal personalization. Explore Learning to Rank (LTR) algorithms (pointwise, pairwise, listwise) and the integration of complex deep learning models for fine-grained scoring. Understand the crucial role of real-time feature stores (e.g., Feast, Tecton) for managing and serving features consistently during training and inference.
